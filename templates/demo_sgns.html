What to Look For
  • Words that appear in similar contexts should have high similarity scores
  • Larger embedding dimensions capture more nuances but need more data
  • Larger window sizes capture broader context relationships
  • More epochs = better training but takes longer
  • More negative samples = better discrimination but slower training
