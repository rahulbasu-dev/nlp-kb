TF-IDF: Statistical Word Importance

Learn how TF-IDF measures word importance using simple formulas

What is TF-IDF?

TF-IDF (Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency) is a statistical measure that evaluates how important a word is to a document in a collection of documents.

Very Fast
No training needed
Interpretable
Explainable results
Document Search
Perfect for retrieval

The Formula

TF-IDF = TF × IDF
Term Frequency (TF)
TF = (word count in document) / (total words in document)

Measures how often a word appears in a document

Inverse Document Frequency (IDF)
IDF = log(total documents / documents with word)

Gives bonus to rare words across all documents

Key Concepts

Words like "the", "is", "a" appear in almost every document, so their IDF is very low. This is good! These words don't help distinguish documents from each other.

Words that appear in only a few documents get high IDF values. These are the distinctive words that help identify what a document is about.

TF-IDF is ideal for search engines and document retrieval. You can quickly find documents that are most relevant to a query by comparing TF-IDF vectors.
