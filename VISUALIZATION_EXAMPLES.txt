Input Corpus:
  "the cat sat on the mat"
  "the dog played in the park"
  "the bird flew over the tree"

Output Chart:
  Semantic Clusters

              flew •
                |
  sat •----on---+---park • (position words)
      |         |
    cat • dog • bird • (animal words)
      |
    the • in • (prepositions)

What it shows:
  • Animals cluster: cat, dog, bird (similar context)
  • Positions cluster: on, in, park (where things are)
  • "the" separate: common across all (different role)

Learned Relationships:
  cat ←→ dog  : 0.845 (high similarity)
  dog ←→ bird : 0.812 (high similarity)
  cat ←→ bird : 0.788 (high similarity)
  sat ←→ flew : 0.721 (action verbs)

Interactivity:
  🖱️  Hover: Shows word name
  🔍 Zoom: Scroll to explore clusters
  ✋ Pan: Drag to see different areas
  📷 Download: Save visualization
